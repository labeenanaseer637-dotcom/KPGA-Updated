OUR INDUSTRY

A connected industry, from gemstone to finished jewellery.

KPGJA connects the people and businesses who shape the province’s gems and jewellery sector, from gemstone traders and miners to jewellers, lapidaries, artisans, manufacturers and exporters. Each plays a part in bringing local materials and specialist skills to finished products and wider markets. Explore the categories below to learn how these communities contribute to trade, craftsmanship, employment and value addition across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.
