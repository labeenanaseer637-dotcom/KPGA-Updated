THE ASSOCIATION

Connecting people and businesses across the gems and jewellery sector.

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Gems & Jewellery Association (KPGJA) is a representative organisation dedicated to developing, promoting and strengthening the province’s gems and jewellery sector. It brings together gemstone traders, jewellery businesses, lapidaries, artisans, miners, exporters, e-commerce professionals and other industry stakeholders.

KPGJA works to support skills development, employment, value addition, digital transformation, e-commerce, women’s participation and responsible business practices. The association also aims to build stronger connections with local, national and international markets while highlighting the region’s gemstone resources and craftsmanship.

OUR VISION

A skilled, transparent and internationally connected sector.

To build a strong, skilled, transparent, and internationally connected gems and jewellery sector in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa that creates employment, supports entrepreneurship, promotes value addition, and contributes to sustainable economic development.

OUR MISSION

Opportunity and progress across the sector.

  • Promote the gems and jewellery sector and support its businesses, artisans and stakeholders.
  • Encourage modern cutting, polishing, grading, finishing and value addition.
  • Promote e-commerce, digital business and access to national and international markets.
  • Support skills, training, employment, and greater participation by women and youth.
  • Represent the community before institutions and encourage cooperation, transparency and professionalism.

MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT

Building opportunity through a stronger industry.

It is a privilege to lead the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Gems & Jewellery Association as we work alongside the people and businesses that shape our sector. Our province has remarkable gemstone resources and skilled craftspeople. By bringing traders, jewellers, lapidaries, miners, artisans and emerging entrepreneurs together, we can create stronger opportunities across the industry.

We aim to encourage practical skills, thoughtful value addition, digital business and wider access to markets, while supporting meaningful participation by women and young people. Through shared knowledge and cooperation, we can help build a more connected, inclusive and sustainable sector that creates livelihoods and long-term prosperity for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Muhammad Salman NiaziPresident, KPGJA
